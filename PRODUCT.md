# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People in Colombia shopping on a phone, often with one hand, in daylight. They browse desk gear, check the price in COP and the stock, and pay with Wompi. The job on any screen is to choose with confidence and move to the next step without hunting.

## Product Purpose

A focused catalog and checkout. Success is a shopper who can scan products, trust the price and stock, and reach payment without clutter. The shopping app is the primary surface. Brand can show up in how products are presented, but it never outranks the task of choosing and paying.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, clear. Voice is short and specific. The interface should feel like a quiet counter: the product and the price do the talking, and the next step is obvious. Confidence without pressure.

## Anti-references

Dense marketplaces such as Mercado Libre and Amazon: banners, badges, stickers, and visual noise. No false urgency, no discount theater, no popup interruptions.

## Design Principles

1. The product and its price lead. Chrome stays out of the way.
2. One clear next step per screen, sized for a thumb.
3. Trust comes from plain facts: price, stock, and what happens next. Never from urgency.
4. Screens are composed from shared primitives so the catalog and the checkout feel like one product.
5. Copy stays in English, short, and specific.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Comfortable touch targets, visible focus, text contrast that holds on the paper background, and reduced motion when the system requests it. The interface stays in English.
